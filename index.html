A Personal Welcome

Celebrating Seven Years Across Two Worlds

Seven years ago, our paths crossed beneath the porticoes of Bologna during an autumn research colloquium. Between Luca's deep roots in the coastal hills of Liguria and Amara's vibrant Punjabi heritage rooted in Amritsar and London, our lives quickly wove together through shared journeys, rich culinary traditions, and an enduring curiosity for the world.

When we envisioned our wedding, we longed not just for a single afternoon, but for an immersive three-day gathering where our families and dearest friends could truly slow down, savor sunlit terraces, and celebrate together in one timeless sanctuary. We chose Casa Solenne on the quiet northern bluffs of Mallorca for its historic olive groves, terracotta stone courtyards, and sweeping Balearic horizons.

We cannot wait to welcome you to the island this September for three unforgettable days of sunset aperitivos, meaningful vows, joyful dancing, and seaside memories.

Island Discoveries

Our Mallorca Travel Guide

Mallorca's northern coast is renowned for UNESCO heritage mountain ridges, dramatic rocky coves, and quiet historic hamlets.

Scenic Route

Serra de Tramuntana Road

The winding coastal drive between Valldemossa, Deià, and Sóller features sheer cliff drop-offs into the deep blue Mediterranean, stone viewpoints, and ancient olive terraces.

Swimming Cove

Cala Deià Sea Shingle

A natural pebble beach tucked between rocky crags with crystal-clear emerald water. Perfect for an afternoon dip and fresh grilled sea bream at seaside tavernas.

Historic Village

Valldemossa Cobblestones

Stroll the car-free mountain stone lanes filled with bougainvillea, visit the historic royal charterhouse, and taste the famous warm coca de patata potato pastries.

Vintage Railway

Sóller Citrus Tramway

Ride the heritage 1912 wooden tram connecting the citrus valley of Sóller down to the harbor of Port de Sóller through fragrant orange groves.

Arrival & Transit

Destination Travel Details

Everything you need to plan your smooth arrival on Mallorca.

Airport Arrivals (PMI)

All international flights arrive at Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI), which offers direct connections from major European hubs (London, Paris, Frankfurt, Milan, Madrid, Barcelona).

  • 35–45 minutes highway drive from PMI to our partner host hotels.
  • Official airport taxi stands are located outside arrivals (approx. €55–€75).
  • Pre-booked private airport transfers can also be arranged via your hotel concierge.

Car Rental & Mountain Roads

Renting a car at PMI is excellent for daytime island exploration. However, private wedding shuttles are provided for all official weekend events so you can celebrate freely without driving winding mountain roads at night.

  • Rental agencies available directly inside the PMI terminal.
  • Compact vehicles recommended for narrow village lanes.
  • Free guest parking available at each partner hotel.

Ferry & Schengen Entry

For guests traveling across mainland Spain, high-speed passenger ferries operate daily between Barcelona or Valencia and the Port of Palma.

  • Spain is within the European Schengen Zone. Ensure your passport has at least 6 months validity.
  • Check any visa or ETIAS requirements based on your passport issuing country.

The Sanctuary

Casa Solenne Grounds & Spaces

Dating back to the 17th century, Casa Solenne balances historic Balearic stone architecture with verdant terraces overlooking the sea.

Ceremony Lawn

The Cliffside Sea Lawn

Dinner Courtyard

Local Favorites

Our Favorite Coastal Spots

A few of our cherished culinary and swimming spots if you extend your time on the island.

Seaside Dining

Ca's Patró March

Iconic open-air rustic seafood restaurant perched right on the rocky crags of Cala Deià. Grilled sea bass and cold albariño with waves lapping below.

Village Bistro

Es Taller Valldemossa

A former historic mechanic's workshop converted into a luminous tapas kitchen with seasonal Tramuntana produce and natural Balearic wines.

Sunset Viewpoint

Sa Foradada Clifftop

A dramatic natural stone promontory with a hollowed sea arch. Head here an hour before dusk for cold drinks and the most famous sunset in the Balearics.
